What Is a Trading Setup?

STEP 1 · LEARN

A trading setup is a specific combination of conditions that must all be present before a trade is considered. It's not a feeling or a hunch — it's a checklist.

A setup is not created because the market moved. A setup exists only when the required conditions are present.

STEP 2 · SEE
✓Condition 1 — Dominance present
✓Condition 2 — Trend aligned
✕Condition 3 — Confirmation tick
2 / 3 MET — SETUP NOT VALID
