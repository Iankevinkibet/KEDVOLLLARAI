Barrier Check™

RESEARCH SYSTEM
WHAT IS IT?

Barrier Check™ is a visualization layer that overlays potential entry conditions onto live digit activity, so a trader can see where conditions might be forming in real time. It's a research tool for observing conditions, not a strategy that trades on its own.

THE IDEA
  • A barrier zone is defined around a digit range of interest.
  • Live digit activity is plotted against that zone.
  • Entering the zone is a visual cue, not a signal by itself.
  • A confirmation condition is still required before anything is treated as valid.
  • The tool's job is to make conditions visible sooner — not to replace judgment.
