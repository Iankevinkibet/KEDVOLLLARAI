Only Downs

RESEARCH
WHAT IS IT?

Only Downs studies confluence between Bollinger Bands, RSI, and momentum to look for potential downside continuation conditions. All three signals must point the same direction before the setup is even considered.

THE IDEA
  • Price position relative to the Bollinger Bands.
  • RSI reading, and whether it sits in an oversold zone.
  • Momentum direction, confirming the move isn't stalling.
  • A confirming tick after all three align.
  • Entry is only considered once every condition points the same way.

CORRECT ✓

With one of the three conditions no longer aligned, the setup is invalid — the response is to wait for a fresh alignment.

NOT QUITE

All three signals need to agree. If one flips before confirmation, the setup no longer holds.
